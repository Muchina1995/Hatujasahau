Justice to us.

We stand for justice, truth, and accountability. Our mission is to seek justice for innocent lives lost to political violence, police brutality, and abuse of state power. Every life taken unjustly is a wound to our nation, and every unanswered death deepens that injustice. We speak for the silenced, honor the fallen, and demand accountability from those entrusted with authority. Justice is not a favor granted by power — it is a right owed to every citizen. Until truth is acknowledged and justice is served, we will continue to stand, to speak, and to seek a Kenya where human life is respected and the rule of law prevails.

METHODOLOGY

About Hatujasahau

A record of what the government said it would do, and what it actually did — kept so it can't quietly be forgotten.

What we do

We log public promises, spending decisions, and documented incidents, and revisit each one until it has a clear outcome. Every claim on this site links to a primary source: a document, a court record, a verified recording, or independent reporting.

What we don't do

We don't publish unverified accusations, and we separate our own analysis from the underlying facts clearly, so readers can tell the two apart at a glance.
